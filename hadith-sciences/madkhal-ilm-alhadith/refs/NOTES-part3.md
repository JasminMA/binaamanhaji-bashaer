## Source issues
- PDF 47: the transcriber writes «كتابي (لغة المحدث)، و(الإرشادات) للشيخ طارق عوض الله»; rendered as a reading pointer only: «لغة المحدّث» و«الإرشادات» للشيخ طارق عوض الله (no first person).
- PDF 53: the erroneous Qurqasani wording «زوال الدنيا أهون على الله من هذه على أهلها» and the correct «ما على أهل هذه لو انتفعوا بإهابها» are quoted exactly as printed, as the book's example of an error; not checked against hadith collections.
- PDF 56: the first sentence gives the Karmani chain in short («حسان بن إبراهيم الكرماني عن أبي سعيد الخدري»); the page uses the full chain the book gives in the next sentence (عن أبي سفيان عن أبي نضرة عن أبي سعيد).
- PDF 64: in al-Dhahabi's third quote the gloss «-يعني: مخالفًا للقواعد-» was left out of the quotation marks (it is an explanatory insertion, not al-Dhahabi's wording). Al-Mu'allimi's long quote: only its first sentence is quoted verbatim; the rest is given as a paraphrase outside quotation marks.
- PDF 58 and 64: reminder box «لا تنسَ تجديد نيتك…» left out. PDF 66: Telegram line and link, «تمّ تلخيص الكتاب بفضل الله», and the closing praise/dua left out.
- No point in PDF 46b–66 looked wrong enough to drop; no doubtful points from OUTLINE section 5 fall in this range.
- PDF 57 (lesson 5), Jabir b. Samura «علام تومئون بأيديكم كأنها أذناب خيل شمس…»: the source prints «رواه البخاري ومسلم», but the hadith is known from Sahih Muslim, not al-Bukhari. The takhrij is dropped; the hadith text is kept as printed (REVIEW M4).
