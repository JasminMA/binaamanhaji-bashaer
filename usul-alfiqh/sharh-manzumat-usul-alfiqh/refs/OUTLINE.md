## 0. Important: structure of the file

The file is **not one booklet with continuous numbering**. It is **27 separate booklets put together**, titled
«تلخيص شرح منظومة أصول الفقه (1)» to «(27)», all «الشيخ محمد بن صالح العثيمين», all published by «عطاء · البناء المنهجي».
Each booklet has:
- its own cover page (printed page 1, no number shown),
- its own «الفهرس» page (printed p.2),
- content pages whose printed numbers **restart at 3**.

So the printed page number equals the PDF page number **only in booklet 1** (PDF 1-5). Everywhere else: PDF page = (first PDF page of the booklet) + (printed page) − 1.
Each booklet matches one lesson (one recorded session) of the Shaykh's explanation. Most booklets start with a blue "(تتمة)" heading that continues the last topic of the previous booklet.

## 3. One work or several?

**One work**: a summary of Shaykh Ibn Uthaymin's explanation of his own poem «منظومة أصول الفقه وقواعده», split into 27 numbered booklets (1)…(27) that follow the poem in order from its opening (PDF 3) to its last verses (PDF 169, with an explicit closing sentence).
There is no separate «قواعد الفقه» part and no separate second work. Usul material and fiqh-rule material are mixed in the order of the poem. Roughly:
- Introduction and the aims of the Sharia: PDF 3-10
- Rules of ease, need, prohibition, and the base rules for things and acts of worship: PDF 13-35
- Command and prohibition, the Prophet's ﷺ acts, competing benefits and harms, the cause (illa) behind a ruling, conditions and causes, presumption (zann) and doubt: PDF 38-75
- The sources of legal obligation (Qur'an, Sunna, consensus, analogy), intention and legal tricks (hiyal): PDF 78-93
- Liability (daman), contracts, custom (urf), lawsuits (da'awa), trustees, conditions: PDF 96-143
- «قواعد فقهية عامة»: PDF 142-162
- «دلالات الألفاظ»: PDF 161-169

## 6. Things that look wrong in the source

1. **Numbering**: the file is 27 booklets with page numbers restarting at each one (see section 0). Printed page numbers repeat 27 times, so references must use PDF page numbers.
2. **Indexes are incomplete**: each «الفهرس» lists only the blue and green boxes. Many bulleted sub-sections are missing (for example «المفرط في التعلم لا يعذر بجهله» PDF 14, «شروط العبادة المشروعة» PDF 31-34, «الاستصحاب والاستحسان» PDF 84, «شروط القياس» PDF 85). Booklet (1)'s index (PDF 2) lists only 2 entries.
3. **Heading names change between a section and its "(تتمة)"**:
   - «الأصل في الأمر والنهي» (PDF 39) becomes «الأمر والنهي (تتمة)» (PDF 44, 50).
   - «تقديم الأعلى عند تزاحم المصالح» (PDF 51) becomes «تقديم الأعلى في المصالح والأدنى في المفاسد (تتمة)» (PDF 55).
   - «القياس» (PDF 79) becomes «حجة القياس (تتمة)» (PDF 83).
   - «الأصل في العبادات المنع إلا ما أذن به الشرع» (PDF 27) becomes «الأصل في العبادة المنع إلا بإذن الشارع (تتمة)» (PDF 31).
   - The index of booklet (13) (PDF 71) has «الأمر والفروض (تتمة)», but the page (PDF 72) has «الأوامر والفروض (تتمة)».
   - Booklet (21) uses «الدعوى (تتمة)» and booklet (22) uses «الدعاوى (تتمة)» / «الدعاوَى».
4. **"(تتمة)" with no original heading**:
   - «الأحكام (تتمة)» (PDF 61, 67): no blue heading «الأحكام» appears earlier.
   - «الدعوى (تتمة)» (PDF 126): booklet 20 has no blue «الدعوى» heading. The topic started under «العقود (تتمة)».
5. **Verse wording differs between quotations**: the second hemistich of the فروض العين والكفاية verse reads «فذو كفاية أتى» at PDF 69 but «فذو كفاية أُثِر» at PDF 72.
6. **Count that does not match the list**: PDF 154 says «وأما في السنة فجاءت في ستّ مواضع» (also ungrammatical, should be «ستة مواضع»), but only two examples follow.
7. **Odd wording**: PDF 91 says «وعلى رأي بعض جمهور العلماء» ("some of the majority"), which is probably a slip for «جمهور العلماء» or «بعض العلماء».
8. **Reference to check**: PDF 62 cites «(الفروق) للزريراني». The Hanbali al-Zarirani's known work on this subject is titled «إيضاح الدلائل في الفرق بين المسائل», so the short title may be loose.
9. **Repeated verse at a boundary**: PDF 19 repeats the verse «وكل ممنوع فللضرورة يباح والمكروه عند الحاجة» already quoted at PDF 15. This is intentional (the continuation re-quotes it), but it means the verse appears twice.
10. PDF 74 explains «الخلفاء الأربعة» as if it were the verse's wording, but the verse quoted just above has «الراشدين الخلفا».
