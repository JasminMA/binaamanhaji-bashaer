## Source issues

- lecture5 p.7 (printed 6): «وأركان الصلاة ستة: (القيام، الركوع، الاعتدال، السجود، الجلوس بين السجدتين، الجلوس للتشهد الأخير)». Lesson 4 counts thirteen pillars; the six here are the physical (فعلية) ones. The page says «الأركان الفعلية الستة» instead of «أركان الصلاة ستة».
- lecture5 p.4 (printed 3): the text says المتابعة والسبق والتخلف «سيأتي بيانها في موضعها». They come in the same file (pp. 6-8), so the page gives them in their place and leaves the forward reference out.
- lecture5 p.2: the hadith is printed «من صلاته الفذّ». Quoted as printed, with no added source. lecture5 gives no source (takhrij) for any of its hadiths, so they are quoted as «قوله ﷺ».
- lecture5 p.4: «لا صلاة لمنفرد خلف الصف» is followed by «فصلاة المنفرد خلف الصف تصح لكن مع الكراهة». Both are kept as the book gives them.
- lecture5 p.9-10: the seven conditions for the jumu'a to be obligatory include «الإقامة», but the «أهل الكمال» for the forty are described with «مستوطنين» (the six attributes). The book itself distinguishes المقيم from المستوطن, so this is consistent. Noted only.
- lecture6 p.3 (printed 2): the two estimates of the distance are given as printed («مئة وأربعة وأربعين كيلومترات»، «أربعة وثمانين كيلومترات»), with «لا بأس من الأخذ بكلا القولين».
- lecture6 p.6: the shorter Shafi'i funeral du'a is quoted only up to «…ظلمة القبر» and then «إلى آخره». It is not completed.
- lecture6 p.6: «التسليمة الثانية» is listed among the sunan. The page adds «والسلام (الأول) ركن», which follows from the seventh pillar «السلام». Remove the parenthesis if the reviewer prefers.
- lecture6 p.8 and p.10: the gold nisab is 85 g of pure gold (24 carat), ~97 g at 21 carat and ~113 g at 18 carat. The silver nisab is 595 g. All copied as printed.
- lecture6 p.8: the nisab list (camels 5, cows 30, sheep 40, crops 5 awsuq = 300 sa' or 1200 mudd) is given as the book states it. The detailed tables are in lecture7.
- I left nothing else out as wrong.
