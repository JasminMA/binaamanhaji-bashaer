## Looks wrong / verify (only the qawaid items matter here)
- shamil-qawaid PDF 8, القاعدة «هل العبرة بالألفاظ أو بالمعاني»: says the Shafi'is generally give priority to the wording, and that the معتمد for «وهبتك هذا الثوب بكذا» is the rules of هبة. In the madhhab, هبة بثواب معلوم is normally treated as بيع (اعتبارًا بالمعنى), so the ruling looks reversed. **Verify.** PDF 9 (سلَم بلفظ البيع → بيع على المعتمد) is consistent with the madhhab.
- shamil-taahil PDF 49, الحال الرابعة of المطلق والمقيد (same cause, different ruling; tayammum/wudu' elbows): says «يُقيَّد عند الشافعية». Many Shafi'i usul texts say there is no حمل in this case, and the elbows in tayammum are by qiyas. **Verify** the wording.
- shamil-taahil PDF 7, definition of المانع: «ولا يلزم من عدمه وجود ولا عدم» without «لذاته» (the شرط definition has it). Minor.
- taahil4 PDF 3: «فلا قل لهما» (verse typo).
- shamil-qawaid PDF 6: lists الخلع and المفاداة (with money) among the صرائح of طلاق, and states that a ميت's wudu' needs a نية but his ghusl does not («لغز»). Both are fine points of the madhhab; check them before stating them flatly.
- Two different nazms of the five qawa'id are attributed: «الدكتور علي القديمي» (qawaid PDF 5) and «العلامة الأهدل» (PDF 11). Not an error, but keep the attributions straight.
