# Possible enhancements

Ideas for improving the مُدارسات study site, gathered after adding the لغة المحدث course (November 2026). Items marked ★ are the suggested first steps.

## Maintainability

1. ✅ **Done — shared lecture CSS.** All 26 lecture pages now link `assets/lecture.css` instead of carrying their own ~9 KB `<style>` copy, so a design change touches one file. Rendering verified pixel-identical before and after.
2. ✅ **Done — page templates.** The shared head, breadcrumb and previous/next bars now live once in `src/templates/`. Each lesson is only its body (`src/…/lectureN.html`) plus a title in the course's `course.json`, and `build.ps1` generates the pages (needs nothing installed; `-Check` finds stale pages). I used a small PowerShell generator instead of Jekyll or Eleventy because the machine has no Ruby or Node and the pages must keep opening straight from disk. See `src/README.md`.
3. **Unify the two breadcrumb styles.** Index pages use `nav.tabs`, while lecture pages use long inline `style="…"` attributes. One class would do. Now a one-file change in `src/templates/breadcrumb.html`, `crumb-link.html` and `crumb-current.html`.
4. ✅ **Done — `.gitattributes`.** Text files are LF everywhere (`* text=auto eol=lf`), PDFs/PNGs are binary and the `.txt` transcripts stay byte for byte, so the LF/CRLF warnings are gone and the generated pages and the stale-page check behave the same on Windows and on GitHub. 21 working files that Windows had turned into CRLF were converted back to LF; nothing in the repository itself changed.

## Repository organisation

5. ✅ **Done — loose source files tidied.** The western-thought transcript PDF and `.txt` moved into `islamic-culture/western-thought/refs/`, like the other two courses, and the two links on that course page now point there. The site check confirms every link still resolves.
6. **Large PDFs.** The repo now holds about 30 MB of PDFs, and every clone downloads them. Git LFS or hosting them elsewhere (such as Google Drive or a GitHub release) would keep the repo light.
7. **Missing source.** The نظم المعين lesson 1 transcript isn't in `refs/`.

## Studying features

8. ✅ **Done — quiz scores.** `assets/quiz.js` adds a floating score bar to every quiz (a dot per question, running count, final "نتيجتك: ٦ من ٨" with a remark). The first answer counts and the other options lock; reset starts over. Pages still work without JavaScript, nothing is stored or sent, and the site check now scans scripts and enforces the quiz markup. See `src/README.md`.
9. ✅ **Done — progress tracking.** Lesson pages have a "done" toggle; course pages show a progress bar, a ✓ badge on finished lessons, the last lesson opened and a clear button. Stored in the browser only (lesson numbers, nothing else), validated as untrusted data, and hidden when storage is blocked. Pages work without JavaScript. See `src/README.md`.
10. ✅ **Done — comparative glossary.** `hadith-sciences/glossary/` compares ten terms (المنكر, الشاذ, المضطرب, المرسل, المسند, الحسن, الغريب, التدليس, التفرّد, الاعتبار) across the two courses: an overview map of which lesson teaches what, a diagram of the two teaching routes, and a card per term showing each teacher side by side with links, a shared core and the difference. Built only from the lesson summaries. The two teachers agree on substance; the differences are order, counting and emphasis, and the page says so. Linked from the stage page and both course pages. See `src/README.md`.
11. ✅ **Done — flashcard review.** `review/` turns the lessons into a spaced-repetition deck of 431 cards, cut automatically from what the lessons already say: every multiple-choice question (answer plus explanation), every open Q&A, and the bold points of each «خلاصة الدرس» (heading → rest, or the sentence with the bold words blanked). Pick a course or lesson, reveal the answer, say "تذكّرتها" or "لم أتذكّرها"; remembered cards return after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days, forgotten ones come back in the same session and tomorrow. Sessions are at most 20 cards. Only card ids and due dates are kept, in the browser; it works offline and with storage blocked. The deck is generated by `build.ps1` (so it follows lesson edits and `-Check` catches staleness), a "مراجعة" link is in every top bar, and the check validates the deck and the page (16 injected faults all caught). Tested in Edge: phone and desktop, light and dark, tampered/invalid/blocked storage. Western-thought has no «خلاصة» section, so its cards come from its Q&A only. See `src/README.md`.
12. **Full poem pages.** Show the whole نظم المعين and لغة المحدث texts on one page each for memorisation, with verse numbers, and maybe audio if recordings exist.
13. ✅ **Done — site search.** `search/` searches every lesson, course page and the glossary at once, offline too. `build.ps1` generates a section-by-section index (about 300 entries); matching ignores diacritics and hamza/ta-marbuta/ya variants and works inside words ("منكر" finds "المنكر"); results show the page, the section and a highlighted snippet, and open the exact section (every `<section>` now has an id). A "بحث" link is in every top bar. The check verifies that every index entry points at a real page and anchor. Tested in Edge, light and dark, at phone width and with the server stopped. See `src/README.md`.

## Design and accessibility

15. ✅ **Done — dark mode for diagrams.** The 21 fixed-colour diagrams (inside `.dia` panels) are no longer a bright white box in dark mode: a single CSS rule inverts them while keeping the hues. Looking for the cause also exposed 16 diagrams in the لغة المحدث lessons whose white badge labels became nearly unreadable on the light dark-mode badge colours; they now turn dark. Light mode is pixel-identical on the pages compared, and the site check warns if a new fixed-colour diagram is added outside a `.dia` panel. See `src/README.md`.
17. **Mobile diagrams.** Wide diagrams need sideways scrolling on phones. Key ones could get a stacked mobile version, or a "view full size" option.
18. ✅ **Done — skip link and focus styles.** Every page starts with a "تخطَّ إلى المحتوى" link that appears on the first Tab and jumps past the breadcrumb and hero; a visible 3px focus ring now shows on every link, button and card in light and dark mode (two rules used to remove it); lesson pages and index pages have a `<main>` landmark; card lift stops under "reduce motion". The site check fails if any of this is missing or an outline is removed. See `src/README.md`.
19. ✅ **Done — self-hosted fonts.** IBM Plex Sans Arabic and Reem Kufi now come from `assets/fonts/` (8 `.woff2` files, about 226 KB, Arabic and Latin subsets only, licences included). Every page links `assets/fonts.css`; there is no request to Google any more, and the site check rejects any outside host. Verified in Edge with the network fully blocked: all faces load locally and the pages look the same. Opening a page from disk in Firefox may use the fallback font. This is also the first step of #23. See `src/README.md`.

## Quality checks

20. ✅ **Done — automatic checks on GitHub.** `tools/check-site.ps1` (stale generated pages, structure and RTL/accessibility, tag balance, duplicate ids, broken links and fragments, orphan pages, shared CSS, security rules) runs on every push to `main` and every pull request via `.github/workflows/checks.yml`. It also runs locally with no installs. I injected 25 deliberate faults into a copy of the site and every one was caught. See `src/README.md`.
21. **Proofread the لغة المحدث verses against the printed book.** The transcription has 164 verses against the book's 165, so one line may be missing.

## Discoverability

22. ✅ **Done — share previews, descriptions and favicon.** Every page (26 lessons plus the 7 hand-written pages) now has a description, the Open Graph block, a favicon and a theme colour; a 1200x630 card image shows in WhatsApp/Telegram previews. Lesson descriptions come from the lesson cards via `course.json` and are required by the build; the site check verifies the whole block and the image. See `src/README.md`.
23. ✅ **Done — offline support (PWA).** After one visit the whole site (52 files, 2.5 MB: pages, styles, scripts, fonts, icons) is kept in the browser, so it opens with no connection, and it can be added to a phone home screen. `sw.js` is generated by the build with a content-hash version, so updates swap in cleanly and the check fails if it is stale. Tested in Edge over http: first visit caches 52 files, with the server stopped a never-visited lesson, a folder address and the progress button all work, an uncached link shows a friendly Arabic page, and a changed file replaces the old cache. The PDFs/transcripts still need internet. See `src/README.md`.

## Added during the work

24. ✅ **Done — quiz-answer-check skill and `tools/list-quizzes.ps1`.** The skill re-checks every marked answer against the lesson, then the transcript in `refs/`, checks the wrong options and the explanation, and reports without editing. The tool lists all 161 multiple-choice and about 150 open questions with answers and prints bias statistics. It already shows that the marked answer is also the longest option in 142 of 161 questions (88%), and that it sits in 2nd place in 72 of 161, so the quizzes are easier to guess than they should be. **Review progress:** "معالم الخطاب المدني" (42 questions) and "لغة المحدث" (98 questions) have been checked answer by answer against the transcripts; two wording errors were fixed (a reversed relation in the Maalat lecture 3 Q4, and "تدليس التحميل" for "التجميل" in لغة المحدث lecture 7), and the answer positions are now balanced in both courses. The marked answer is still the longest option in about 69% of لغة المحدث questions (was about 88%). "نظم المعين" (83 questions, lecture 1 against the lesson only because its transcript is missing) has been checked too: all marked answers were right; one wording was aligned with the transcript («ترجيح حال الحديث» became «ترجيح الخلاف في الحديث» in lecture 3 and the course index), and positions are balanced, but the marked answer is still the longest option in about 81% of that course's questions (the gaps are smaller). "تاريخ الفكر الغربي" (150 open Q&As) was checked last, by looking up every date, name, number and list in the transcript text; no errors found and nothing changed (the text version of that transcript is scrambled, so only specifics that survive extraction were confirmed that way; reasoning-style answers were read against the lessons). **All four courses have now been through the quiz-answer-check pass.**
25. **Stop committing generated pages (deploy with a GitHub Action).** Today every lesson exists twice in git: the source in `src/` and the page `build.ps1` generates, because Pages deploys from the branch with no build step. Switching Pages to an Action that runs `build.ps1` and publishes the result would let the generated pages, `sw.js` and the search index stay out of git and halve the diffs. Costs: change the Pages source in the repository settings, add a deploy workflow, and the site is no longer viewable straight from the branch. Deferred by choice; the current setup is simple and `build.ps1 -Check` catches drift.
